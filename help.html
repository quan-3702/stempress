How to use StemPress

1. Add a song

Click ＋ Add songs or drop an MP3, WAV, M4A, FLAC, OGG or a video (MP4, MOV, WEBM) on the window. Songs can be up to 15 minutes long. The first time, the AI model (about 285 MB) is downloaded once; after that StemPress works offline.

With a graphics card (most computers from the last years) a 4-minute song takes about 1–3 minutes. Without one it runs on the processor and takes longer. You can keep using the computer meanwhile.

2. Listen and mix

Every track has M (mute), S (solo), a volume slider and – in Pro – left/right. 🎤 Karaoke mutes the vocals, 🗣 Vocals only keeps just the singing. Drag on the waveform to loop a hard part, and slow it down with Speed – the pitch stays the same.

Keys: Space play/pause, ←/→ 5 seconds back/forward.

3. Save

⬇ Save stems / mix saves the tracks you choose as MP3 – or in Pro as WAV 16/24-bit for your music software, your own mix, with the new key and speed, or everything in one ZIP.

Tempo, key and chords

StemPress finds the tempo (BPM) and key of every song. Pro shows the chords as the song plays and can move the whole song to another key (🎼 Key → Apply).
