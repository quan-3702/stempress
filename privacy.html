StemPress – Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026. StemPress is made by NTQ37.

Your music stays on your computer

The AI that separates the instruments runs on your own computer. Your songs, the separated tracks, mixer settings, tempo, key and chords are stored only in this app on your device. They are never uploaded to NTQ37 or anyone else.

What is downloaded

The first time you separate a song, the app downloads the open-source AI model (HTDemucs, about 285 MB) from Hugging Face and the AI engine library from jsDelivr. These downloads contain no information about you or your music. After that the app works offline.

Copyright

Use StemPress only with music you own or have the right to use. Separated tracks are for your own practice, study and projects allowed by the rights holder.

Purchases

StemPress Pro is bought through the Microsoft Store. NTQ37 does not receive your payment details; the app only asks the Store whether Pro is owned.

No accounts, no tracking

There is no account, no advertising and no analytics.
